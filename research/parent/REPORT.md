The two additional candidate areas are powerful numbers and Siegel zeros.
For powerful numbers, one exact target is the existence or nonexistence of three
consecutive positive integers all of whose prime divisors occur to exponent at
least two. Recent papers by
[Chan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21485) and
[Ma](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23418) describe restricted nonexistence results.
Their restrictions must not be dropped. The
[2026 arithmetic-progression paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06697) addresses
progressions with a varying common difference; being consecutive in the ordered
sequence of powerful numbers does not mean being consecutive integers.

For Siegel zeros, a formal target must specify the zero-free region, constants,
character conditions, and conductor range. The current review has not fixed that
statement or established a feasible route. Conditional implications from another
